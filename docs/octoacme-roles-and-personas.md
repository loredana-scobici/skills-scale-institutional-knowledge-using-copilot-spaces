# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide the technical approach and help the engineering team deliver reliable solutions. They make or coordinate technical decisions while keeping implementation aligned with product goals and project constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Coordinate engineering estimates and identify technical dependencies
- Support Developers with implementation questions and code quality
- Surface technical risks, trade-offs, and mitigation options

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs against product priorities
- Keep the Project Manager informed about technical dependencies, risks, and changes that may affect delivery plans

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance or Test Leads coordinate the approach to verifying that a solution meets its requirements and is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test scope, strategy, and quality expectations with the team
- Coordinate functional, integration, and regression testing
- Track defects and communicate quality risks and release-readiness evidence
- Identify opportunities to improve test coverage and prevention of defects

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers to plan testability, automate checks, and investigate defects
- Align with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria and expected user outcomes
- Report quality status, unresolved defects, and release risks to the Project Manager so they can be reflected in project coordination and status updates

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX or Design Leads guide research and experience design so that delivered solutions meet user needs and are usable and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize user needs
- Create and validate interaction designs, prototypes, and accessibility considerations
- Maintain design guidance and communicate experience decisions
- Identify usability risks and propose ways to validate design assumptions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs, product goals, and backlog priorities
- Work with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads to assess feasibility and ensure the implementation reflects the intended experience
- Coordinate research, design reviews, and validation milestones with the Project Manager

---

## Business Sponsors / Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors or Stakeholder Representatives provide business context, clarify desired outcomes, and represent stakeholder interests in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate business goals, constraints, and measures of success
- Identify relevant stakeholders and provide timely input or decisions
- Clarify decision authority and escalate unresolved business trade-offs
- Review progress and confirm whether outcomes meet business needs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on customer and business value, priorities, and outcome measures
- Provide the Project Manager with stakeholder input, decisions, and timely responses to escalations
- Consult with Developers and Engineering / Technical Leads when business constraints or technical trade-offs affect the solution

---

## Delivery Facilitators / Scrum Masters (where applicable)

### Role Summary
Delivery Facilitators or Scrum Masters support the team's delivery practices, facilitate team ceremonies, and help address workflow impediments. This role is used when the team benefits from dedicated facilitation.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives as agreed by the team
- Help the team identify and resolve workflow impediments
- Encourage continuous improvement and make workflow issues visible
- Support effective collaboration without taking ownership of product scope or project reporting

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on delivery flow and impediments while keeping project plans and stakeholder reporting with the Project Manager
- Work with the Product Manager and Developers to support clear planning, backlog conversations, and team feedback
- Escalate cross-team blockers through the Project Manager and bring recurring team-process issues to retrospectives

---

## Applying Roles to Different Team Setups
- Treat these personas as areas of responsibility, not mandatory separate positions; one person may cover multiple roles, or a role may be shared, depending on project size and team structure.
- Make decision ownership and escalation paths explicit at project kickoff, especially when responsibilities are combined.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
